Sheet Pan Salmon, Zero Drama

Seafood30 min totalserves 4

Salmon and broccolini roasted on one pan with lemon and paprika. Twenty-five minutes, one pan, zero drama.

Before we start

This is the one you make when you're tired and still want to feel like you have your life together. It works every time. The broccolini goes in first so its edges get crispy, and the salmon joins it for the last few minutes. Line the pan and cleanup is basically a myth.

Ingredients

  • 4skin-on salmon fillets6 oz each
  • 1 lbbroccolinitrimmed
  • 2lemonsone sliced, one for juice
  • 3garlic clovesthinly sliced
  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • 1 tspsmoked paprika
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F and line a sheet pan.
  2. Toss the broccolini with olive oil, garlic and a pinch of salt, and spread it on the pan.
  3. Roast the broccolini alone for 8 minutes. It gets a head start. It's earned it.
  4. Push the broccolini to the edges, add the salmon skin-side down, and season with paprika, salt and pepper.
  5. Lay lemon slices over the fish and roast 10 to 12 minutes, until the salmon flakes at the thickest part.
  6. Squeeze the rest of the lemon over everything and serve straight from the pan. Plates are optional. I won't tell.

Kitchen's closed The crispy broccolini tips are the cook's portion. Eat two before you call everyone to the table.
