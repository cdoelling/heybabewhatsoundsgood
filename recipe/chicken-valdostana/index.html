Chicken Valdostana, Dressed for the Alps

Italian95 min totalserves 4cozy weather

Crispy chicken under prosciutto, sage and melted Fontina, with roasted delicata and mushroom pan sauce. Fancy, but secretly a sheet pan.

Before we start

This looks like something a waiter spends a full minute describing. It's really a skillet and a sheet pan taking turns. The one rule: don't wash the skillet after the chicken. Everything stuck to it becomes your sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb. delicata squash (2 to 3 squash)halved lengthwise, seeded, and cut into ½-inch-thick half-moons, acorn squash works too
  • 5 tbspolive oildivided
  • 2½ tsp. kosher saltdivided, plus more to taste
  • 1½ tsp. black pepperdivided, plus more to taste
  • 4(10-oz.) bonelessskinless chicken breasts
  • ½ cupplus 2 tsp. all-purpose flourdivided
  • 3 tbspcold unsalted buttercubed and divided
  • 8thin prosciutto di Parma or prosciutto cotto slicesabout 3 oz.
  • 12fresh sage leavesplus more thinly sliced for garnish
  • 4 ozFontina cheesethinly sliced
  • 8 ozcremini mushroomsthinly sliced
  • ⅓ cupdry white wine
  • ½ cupchicken broth
  • Flaky sea saltto taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F with a rack in the lower third. Line a large baking sheet with foil and put it in the oven for 10 minutes. Take it out and toss the squash right on the hot pan with 2 tablespoons oil, ¾ teaspoon salt and ¾ teaspoon pepper. Spread it in a single layer and bake until browned underneath, about 20 minutes. Flip the squash and push it to one side. The hot pan is the trick. It sears the bottoms the second they land.
  2. Meanwhile, season the chicken with 1½ teaspoons salt and ½ teaspoon pepper. Put ½ cup flour in a shallow bowl and dredge the chicken until evenly coated, shaking off the extra.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons oil and 1 tablespoon butter in a large skillet over medium. Brown the chicken on both sides without cooking it through, 8 to 12 minutes, then move it to the empty side of the baking sheet. Do not clean the skillet. I'm serious. Top each breast with 2 slices of prosciutto, then the sage leaves, then the Fontina.
  4. Back into the oven it all goes. Bake until an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part of the chicken reads 160°F and the squash is tender, 18 to 20 minutes. Rest 5 minutes; the chicken coasts up to 165°F on its own.
  5. Meanwhile, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon oil in that gloriously dirty skillet over medium. Add the mushrooms with the remaining ¼ teaspoon salt and ¼ teaspoon pepper and cook, stirring now and then, until browned, about 6 minutes. Stir in the remaining 2 teaspoons flour and cook, stirring constantly, until lightly browned, about 1 minute. Add the wine and scrape up the browned bits until it's reduced by half, about 1 minute. Add the broth and reduce by half again, 3 to 4 minutes. Off the heat, whisk in the remaining 2 tablespoons butter until glossy. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Divide the chicken and squash among plates and spoon the mushroom sauce over the chicken. Sprinkle the squash with flaky salt and scatter sliced sage on top.

Kitchen's closed Delicata skin is edible, so don't bother peeling it. Life is short and delicata is bumpy.
