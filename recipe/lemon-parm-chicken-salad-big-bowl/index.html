Lemon-Parm Chicken Salad, Big Bowl

American30 min totalserves 4warm weather

Chicken, quinoa and massaged kale in a sharp lemon vinaigrette, with Parm, almonds and raisins. It needs a bigger bowl than you think.

Before we start

The quinoa and chicken soak up the vinaigrette like sponges, so don't be shy with it. You need a truly enormous bowl for this. A regular mixing bowl won't cut it, so grab your big pasta pot if you have to. Yes, really.

Ingredients

Quinoa

  • ½ cupquinoarinsed
  • ¾ tspreduced-sodium chicken Better Than Bouillonoptional

Lemon Vinaigrette

  • 3lemons
  • ¾ cupextra-virgin olive oil
  • 1½ heaping tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsphoney
  • ¼heaping teaspoon dried thyme or oregano
  • ¼heaping teaspoon garlic powder
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

Salad

  • 1(16-ounce) bag chopped kaletough stems removed
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 3 cupshredded cooked chicken
  • 10 ozshredded carrots
  • 1 cupfreshly grated Parmesan cheese (4 ounces)plus more for serving
  • 1 cupgolden raisins or dried cranberries
  • 1 cuproasted salted almonds
  • ½ bunchsoft green herbssuch as fresh basil, parsley, and/or cilantro, for topping, optional
  • 1lemonfor serving, optional

Instructions

  1. Make the quinoa. In a small pot, combine the quinoa, Better Than Bouillon (if using) and ¾ cup water. Bring to a boil over high heat, then turn the heat down to low, cover, and cook until all the liquid has been absorbed, 13 to 15 minutes. Take it off the heat and let it stand, covered, for 5 minutes. Uncover and fluff with a fork.
  2. While that cooks, make the vinaigrette. Using a Microplane, grate the zest of 1 lemon into a jar with a lid. Cut all 3 lemons in half and squeeze in the juice. Add the olive oil, mustard, honey, thyme, garlic powder, ¾ teaspoon salt and some pepper. Close the jar and shake hard until combined, about 30 seconds. Taste and adjust the flavors however you like.
  3. Make the salad. Put the kale in your gigantic bowl and season with a big pinch each of salt and pepper. Massage the kale with your hands until it JUST starts to feel damp. Squeeze it between your fists about 10 times. If you find any thick stem pieces, toss them. Add the cooked quinoa, chicken, carrots, Parm, raisins and about two-thirds of the vinaigrette. Toss it all together. Add more dressing until the greens are coated the way you like.
  4. Chop the almonds and herbs (if using). If you're using the lemon, cut it into 6 wedges. Put as much salad as you like in a shallow bowl and top with the almonds. Garnish with more Parm and the chopped herbs, and serve with the lemon wedges for squeezing.

Kitchen's closed Meal prepping? Go light on the dressing at first, since the quinoa drinks it up. Store the salad, extra dressing, almonds and lemon wedges in separate airtight containers in the fridge for up to 5 days, and add a little dressing to each serving.
