Miso Peanut Chicken Bowls, Slaw Included

Asian30 min totalserves 4

Saucy miso peanut chicken over fluffy rice with a tangy cilantro lime slaw. Thirty minutes, one very good reason to own miso.

Before we start

The sauce is the whole show, so taste it at the end and fix it. More vinegar for tang, more soy for salt, more sriracha for heat. Peanut butter and miso are stubborn, so use a fork to smoosh them into the stock. Whisk it off the heat, or you'll be chasing clumps.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups white rice (any kind!)rinsed
  • 4 cuplow-sodium chicken stockor BTB + water, see page 21
  • 1 tbspunsalted butter
  • ½ cuppeanut butter
  • ¼ cuplow-sodium soy sauceplus more if needed
  • 3 tbspwhite miso paste
  • 2 tbspmaple syrup
  • 2 tbsprice vinegarplus more if needed
  • 1to 2 tablespoons srirachaplus more if needed
  • ½ tspgarlic powder
  • 1½ to 2 pounds bonelessskinless chicken breasts
  • ½ bunchcilantro
  • 1or 2 limesor 4 more tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 10 ozshredded green cabbage or coleslaw mix
  • 1 tbspsesame oiltoasted or not
  • 1 tbspneutral oil
  • Kosher salt
  • Toasted sesame seeds or chopped peanutsfor serving

Instructions

  1. In a small pot, combine the rice, 2½ cups of the stock, and the butter. Bring to a boil over high heat, then turn the heat down to low, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Take it off the heat and let it stand, covered, for 10 minutes, or until you're ready to serve. Fluff with a fork before serving.
  2. In a large saucepan or wide pot, off the heat, whisk together the remaining 1½ cups stock, the peanut butter, soy sauce, miso paste, maple syrup, vinegar, sriracha, and garlic powder. Use a fork to smoosh the peanut butter and miso into the liquid if you need to. Bring to a boil over high heat, then turn the heat down to medium-low.
  3. Trim any white fat off the chicken, then cut it into 2-inch cubes. Add the chicken to the pan with the sauce in an even layer, cover, and cook for 5 minutes. Flip the pieces, cover, and cook until the chicken is cooked through, 5 to 8 minutes more. Pull a piece apart with two forks to check. It should shred easily.
  4. Turn the heat up to medium-high and cook, uncovered, stirring often, until the sauce thickens, 3 to 4 minutes. It's fine if the chicken shreds a bit. If you have time, shred it more with two forks so it soaks up the sauce. Taste, then add more vinegar for tang, soy sauce for salt, and/or sriracha for heat.
  5. Meanwhile, finely chop the cilantro leaves and tender stems and put them in a large bowl. Halve 1 lime and squeeze in the juice. Add the cabbage, sesame oil, neutral oil, and a big pinch of salt. Toss to combine. Add the juice from the second lime if you like it tangy. Go on, be bold.
  6. Scoop the rice into bowls. Top with the chicken and slaw. Drizzle extra sauce from the chicken over the top. Finish with sriracha and toasted sesame seeds.

Kitchen's closed Use up your leftover miso in the miso-creamed spinach and mushrooms on page 126, so it doesn't go to the back of the fridge to be forgotten.
