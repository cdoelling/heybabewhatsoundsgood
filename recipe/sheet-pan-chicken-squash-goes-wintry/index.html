Sheet Pan Chicken, Squash Goes Wintry

American40 min totalserves 6cozy weather

Butter-roasted chicken thighs and delicata squash with dates, walnuts and goat cheese, all on a sheet pan. Yes, the "30 minutes" is a little optimistic.

Before we start

Spread everything in an even layer on both pans. Crowded pans steam instead of roast, and nobody wants sad squash. Toss the spices and butter in properly, too. Really get in there with your hands.

Ingredients

  • 2small delicata squash
  • 1large red onion
  • 2 lbbone-inskin-on chicken thighs
  • 8thyme sprigs
  • 5 tbspunsalted buttermelted
  • 3 tbspmaple syrup
  • 2 tspkosher salt
  • 2 tspground cumin
  • 1 tspcurry powder
  • ½ tspground ginger
  • ½ cuphalved pitted datespreferably Medjool
  • ½ cupraw walnut halves
  • 2big handfuls of arugula
  • 4 ozgoat cheese
  • 4 ozpomegranate seeds1 cup
  • Balsamic vinegarfor serving

Instructions

  1. Put racks in the top and bottom thirds of the oven and preheat to 450°F. If your oven is wide enough for two baking sheets on one rack, use a rack in the middle instead.
  2. Trim the ends off the squash and halve them lengthwise. Scrape out the seeds with a large spoon and toss them. Cut the squash into ½-inch-thick slices. Halve the onion through the root end, then cut it into 1-inch wedges. The layers will fall apart. That's fine, just keep them together if you can.
  3. Pile the chicken, squash, onion and thyme sprigs onto a baking sheet. Add the melted butter, maple syrup, salt, cumin, curry powder and ginger. Toss to coat well. Move half of it to a second baking sheet and spread everything out in an even layer on both pans.
  4. Roast for about 25 minutes, until the thickest part of the chicken hits 165°F inside. Swap the pans between the top and bottom racks halfway through. Pull the pans out and flip the squash, or don't, I won't tell. Scatter the dates and walnuts on top and roast 3 to 4 minutes more, until warm and toasty.
  5. Toss everything together on one of the baking sheets. Scatter the arugula, goat cheese and pomegranate seeds over the top and drizzle with balsamic. Serve straight from the pan.

Kitchen's closed Serve it right off the pan and save yourself a platter to wash. Want boneless, skinless thighs instead? Roast as directed, but rest them under foil if they finish before the squash.
